## AI Chatbots

- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) - Everyday writing, research and images. Free plan, paid from $8/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6yZNUnrpnO0)
- [Claude](https://claude.ai) - Long writing, documents and coding. Free plan, Pro $20/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0HX5E3lGF1o)
- [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com) - Best if you live in Gmail, Docs and Drive. Free plan, Google AI Pro $19.99/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=piKbO5Ne1FQ)
- [Grok](https://grok.com) - Real-time news and posts from X. Free plan, paid from $10/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ASb16MAls4Q)
- [DeepSeek](https://chat.deepseek.com) - Free AI chat for everyday questions, coding help and studying. Free web and app, no subscription (API is pay-as-you-go). [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6yZNUnrpnO0)

## AI Avatar Video

- [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com) - Talking-avatar videos and video translation. Free plan (3 videos/mo), Creator $29/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vJKcw4e7uEI)
- [Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io?via=a1) - Training and explainer videos with AI presenters. Free plan, Starter $19/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vJKcw4e7uEI)

## AI App Builders and Coding

- [Base44](https://base44.com) - Build your first app with no code. Free plan, Starter $20/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7nxH6iR9Asw)
- [Replit](https://replit.com) - Build and host apps with an AI agent. Free plan, Core $25/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyWkeXMe9I4)
- [Lovable](https://lovable.dev) - Websites and web apps from a prompt. Free plan, Pro $25/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vIHqk7kk54g)
- [Cursor](https://cursor.com) - AI code editor for people who already code. Free plan, Pro $20/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mmncNhm05QI)

## AI Music and Voice

- [Suno](https://suno.com) - Full songs with vocals. Free plan (no commercial use), Pro $10/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mgAe2KyiNAU)
- [Udio](https://www.udio.com) - High-quality songs. Free plan, commercial use on Pro $30/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XIHpy7pgy_U)
- [ElevenLabs](https://try.elevenlabs.io/37v43h70lh4r) - Voiceovers, voice cloning and AI songs. Free plan (no commercial use), Starter $6/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cND2UJXcgD4)

## AI Images

- [Leonardo AI](https://leonardo.ai) - Free daily image generation. Free plan (150 tokens/day), Apprentice $12/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MFpXlsfuj2M)
- [Higgsfield](https://higgsfield.ai) - AI images and videos in one plan, with consistent characters. Free trial generations only, Starter $19/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NbaAmD7K_z8)
- [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com) - Best-looking artistic images. No free plan, Basic $10/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NbaAmD7K_z8)

## Automation

- [n8n](https://n8n.io) - Advanced automations and AI agents. Free if self-hosted, cloud from about $24/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UDHsFyki2MA)
- [OttoKit](https://ottokit.com/?aff=5778cef7) - Simple, cheap Zapier alternative. Free plan (250 tasks/mo), Pro $9/mo billed yearly. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LNO-0uqSYio)

## Video Clipping

- [OpusClip](https://opus.pro/?via=b97423) - Turn long videos into Shorts and Reels. Free plan (60 min/mo), Starter $15/mo. [Video review](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3rLdtnru25U)
